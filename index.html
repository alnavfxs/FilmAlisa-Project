Frequently Asked Questions

What is Filmalisa?
Filmalisa is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime, documentaries, and more on thousands of internet-connected devices.
How much does Filmalisa cost?
Watch Filmalisa on your smartphone, tablet, Smart TV, laptop, or streaming device, all for one fixed monthly fee. Plans range from cheap to slightly less cheap.
Where can I watch?
Watch anywhere, anytime. Sign in with your Filmalisa account to watch instantly on the web at filmalisa.com from your personal computer or on any internet-connected device.
How do I cancel?
Filmalisa is flexible. There are no pesky contracts and no commitments. You can easily cancel your account online in two clicks.
What can I watch on Filmalisa?
Filmalisa has an extensive library of feature films, documentaries, TV shows, anime, award-winning Filmalisa originals, and more.
Is Filmalisa good for kids?
The Filmalisa Kids experience is included in your membership to give parents control while kids enjoy family-friendly TV shows and movies in their own space.
